## Geometric proof

`run.py` reopens the registered whole design mesh and calls the pinned native value interface. It requires the compiled module SHA-256, the source-map version, the retained-side rule and the original raw basis. Each row records the native context, physical design vertex and face digests, finite source digest, operation, cap-mask digest and triangulation hash. `match_center_qualification` checks these source identities and the raw fingerprints against the independently recorded physical center before joining its status.

Outward binary64 intervals enclose normalization, canonical coefficient sign, reference-axis selection, the source frame, every represented algebraic graph vertex and the extruded cap. The exact final coordinate rounding is enclosed. Squared-norm sums have a positive summand bound for different reduction orders. For the well-scaled nonzero normals in these records, normalization and its repeated norm check have a uniform relative error below 32 machine epsilons per reduction.
2. **Finite closure.** Over the same box, the projected rim stays simple and encloses all eight corners of the design bounding box with strictly positive distance above the native threshold. Each lower face retains the required winding. All lateral faces stay outside the design projection and all chosen far-cap vertices clear its support bound. The audit checks every earlier rejected cap depth against the same margin. It also bounds the implicit root slope throughout the design box and at the closed operand vertices.
3. **Embedding.** The projected parameter grid and a vertical extrusion give a reference prism. A fixed comparison map takes $(s,t,z)$ to $U(s,t,H(s,t)+z)$, with a continuous piecewise-linear height $H$ interpolated on the same lower triangles. The prism of each reference triangle can be tetrahedralized by joining its centroid to its triangulated boundary. Shared rectangles take the same diagonal and the exterior uses the native cap diagonals. In coordinates scaled to unit side lengths and unit depth, every such tetrahedron has absolute determinant at least 1/3, and all cofactors have magnitude at most 2. If each coordinate of each corner differs by at most $δ$ from its comparison value, the Lipschitz bound for the piecewise-affine error in the infinity norm is $108δ / \min(\mathrm{grid\ spacing},\mathrm{depth})$. The archive also checks $\|U^{-1}\|_∞ < 8$ and bounds the Lipschitz constant of the inverse comparison map by $8(1+\mathrm{Lip}_∞ H)$. A product below one proves injectivity on the whole convex parameter prism. The enclosure includes rounding in the actual affine transform to the physical Boolean frame.
4. **Whole-material intersection.** All fixed faces and moving faces enter an exhaustive, inclusive AABB test. A separated pair stays disjoint. A same-side interval plane test rejects other pairs. For the remaining candidates a fixed vertex cannot touch a moving face, a moving vertex cannot touch a fixed face, and opposing edges cannot touch. Each exclusion uses a strict oriented-volume sign or a certified separating feature box or plane. These are the events through which the incidence of two embedded polyhedral boundaries can change. Internal seam edges, caps and all signed material components participate. A potential zero records its face and local feature, and the verifier can retry at a smaller declared radius.
